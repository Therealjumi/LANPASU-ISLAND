Endpoint scope / review notes

LANPASU E.1-A v018 is a metadata-only baseline redefinition: the original adult geometry is the new age-18 default. The age 0–18 tester labels each year with newborn at the left and default 18 on the right. The compact test GLB deliberately omits other genetic sliders, while the complete v018 master preserves all original genetic morphs, skeleton and animations. No anatomical shape change was made by the age-reference update. The clothing geometry is inherited from v018. The diaper keeps the higher-cut leg openings from v015, but now has more life through a slightly fuller padded front, rounder rear seat, and softer side fullness so it reads more like a real baby diaper instead of a flat garment. The leg elastic and fastening tabs were moved with the shell. The beanie and its rib are tilted farther backward again and shifted slightly more toward the back of the head. The body, face, rig and age-0/age-18 endpoints remain unchanged. All genetic morph targets remain preserved in the v018 full master.
