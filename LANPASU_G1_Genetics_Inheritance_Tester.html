The rules we're protecting

♡ Review the genetics and life-stage rules
  • 63 genes: each numeric child value is between the two parent values, with a triangular midpoint-favored distribution and no mutations.
  • Hair: parents’ tapped Teen+ hair styles (Hair_001–024) already specify their natural 1a–4c textures, no second selection; natural texture inherits between 1a and 4c; newborn face appears first, baby reveals hair color, toddler reveals broad texture (1–4) and starts weight development, child hair presentation and strength develop, teen reveals specific 1a–4c and body type; 18 Kimah is the adult genetics reference.
  • Foundation: 49% Female, 49% Male, 2% Neutral (Intersex) per independent baby.
  • Alignment: one ordered LG → NG → CG → LN → TN → CN → LE → NE → CE scale, midpoint most likely.
  • MBTI: each letter inherits from one parent with 50/50 when letters differ. Movement style inherits 50/50 as one complete style.
  • Siblings: identical children share the same inherited genes and foundation; fraternal children are independent. Mixed triplets are ⅓ paired identical + 1 fraternal and ⅔ all fraternal.
  • Family: in real gameplay, parents must be married; children take their father's surname; pregnancy lasts 280 Alz.
  • Safety: the finished v036 Creator and original Phase G Library are read-only unless you explicitly save a generated child.
